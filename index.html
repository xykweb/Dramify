General Instruction

Thank you for participating in this survey, which takes about 4 minutes to complete and is restricted to participants using desktop or laptop devices only. Careless answers will misguide our research, so please read all the directions and questions carefully before you provide your answers.

This survey comes from Dramify, which is an upcoming streaming platform featuring AI-generated series. Before launch, we would like to better understand viewers’ preferences. We thus invite you to explore our website, discover streaming series that interest you, and share your feedback.

To ensure the best experience, please complete the survey in full-screen mode. After completing the survey, you can press the ESC key to exit full-screen mode. However, please do not exit early, as doing so may significantly affect your survey experience.

If you are ready, enter your Connect ID below and click Next to enter full-screen mode.

In the next section, you will be redirected to the Dramify website, where you will see 16 newly released AI-generated streaming series. Please review the list and select exactly 6 series that you would like to add to your watchlist.

Notes:

  1. You can click the “+” button below each series poster to add it to your Watchlist, and click it again to remove it. The Continue button will appear only when you have exactly 6 series in your Watchlist.
  2. You can click the heart-shaped button below a poster to Like a series, and click the bookmark-shaped button to Save a series. Click either button again to undo that action. You can like and save as many series as you wish; these actions do not count toward your 6 Watchlist selections.
  3. There are no right or wrong answers, so please make your selections based on your genuine feelings. Afterwards, we will ask you some questions about your thoughts when making these selections.
  4. The recommended time to make your selections is 90 seconds. A countdown timer will appear in the left sidebar to help you keep track of the time remaining. However, you may take as long as you need to complete the task, and your response time will not affect your payment.
